  • ✓ Valid Driving License (Minimum 1 year driving history)
  • ✓ Utility Bill Copy (Gas or Electricity bill of current address)
  • ✓ Refundable Security Deposit (Cash or Online transfer, returned upon car return)

Overseas & Tourists

Specially tailored for overseas Pakistanis visiting home.

  • ✓ Original Passport & Visa with entry stamp
  • ✓ International Driving Permit (IDP) or Home Country License
